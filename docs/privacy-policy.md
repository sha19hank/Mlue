# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-06-08

Mlue is an offline-first habit tracker. We believe your personal data belongs exclusively to you. 

Here is exactly how Mlue handles your data:

### 1. What we collect
Nothing. 
We do not track you. We do not use analytics software. We do not use telemetry. We do not use advertisements.

### 2. What we store
Mlue stores your habits, goals, journal entries, and app settings.

### 3. Where your data is stored
All your data is stored locally in a private database on your device.

### 4. Does your data leave your device?
No. Mlue does not sync to any cloud servers. It does not require an internet connection to function. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your information is permanently deleted unless you have enabled Android's native device-level backups.

### 5. Accounts and Sync
There is no account system. There is no cloud sync.

### 6. Permissions
Mlue only requests permissions necessary for core functionality:
- **Alarms & Notifications**: Used strictly to deliver the daily reminders you set locally.

If you have any questions, you can review our full source code on our repository.
